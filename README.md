# NuminOS

NuminOS is an Android app, not an operating system. It puts a small symbol above your other apps. Tap it and a floating window offers a sentence to live with, your practice videos and a meditation, then folds away again.

It has no account and no server. Everything you add stays on your phone. The internet is used only for YouTube titles, thumbnails and videos.

Requires Android 8.0 or newer.

## What it does

- **Sentence:** shows a random line from the books and your own sentences, or one you fix in place. You can like sentences and browse each book.
- **Videos:** keeps your YouTube practice videos in simple categories, with the last one first. One tap opens the video in YouTube.
- **Meditation:** runs a countdown with an opening and closing bell, and keeps going with the screen locked.
- **Symbol:** choose yin-yang, enso, om, dharma wheel or cross.

## Add your own

- **A video:** in the YouTube app, tap Share and choose NuminOS. You can also tap Add in the floating window and paste a link.
- **A sentence:** open the book icon, choose My sentences and tap +. You can also share selected text from any app to NuminOS.
- **Remove something:** long-press a video or one of your sentences.

## Install

Download the APK and open it on your phone. In NuminOS, allow **Show over other apps**.

If Android says this is a restricted setting, open NuminOS in App info, tap the ⋮ menu and choose **Allow restricted settings**.

If the symbol disappears after a while, set the app's battery use to **Unrestricted**. Some phone brands close background apps to save battery.

## Build

You need JDK 17 and the Android SDK.

```sh
./gradlew assembleDebug
```

The APK is written to `app/build/outputs/apk/debug/`.
