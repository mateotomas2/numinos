# NuminOS

NuminOS is an Android app, not an operating system. It puts a small symbol above your other apps. Tap it and a floating window offers a sentence to live with, your practice videos and a meditation, then folds away again.

It has no account and no server. Everything you add stays on your phone. The internet is used only for YouTube titles, thumbnails and videos.

Requires Android 8.0 or newer.

## What it does

- **Sentence:** shows a random line from the books and your own sentences, or one you fix in place. Tap it to open the library. Long-press it to get another, like it or fix it.
- **Videos:** keeps your YouTube practice videos, in categories you create, with the last one first. One tap opens the video in SmartTube if it's installed, otherwise in YouTube.
- **Meditation:** runs a countdown with an opening and closing bell, and keeps going with the screen locked.
- **Themes:** choose yin-yang, enso, om, dharma wheel or cross when you first open the app. Each has its own colors, and most start with a matching book.
- **Settings:** change the theme, choose light or dark (it follows your system by default), set the symbol's opacity and its offset from the edge, or delete everything and start over.

## Add your own

- **A video:** in the YouTube app, tap Share and choose NuminOS. You can also tap + in the floating window and paste a link.
- **A sentence:** tap the sentence, choose My sentences and tap +. You can also share selected text from any app to NuminOS.
- **Remove something:** long-press a video or one of your sentences.

## Install

Download the APK from [Releases](https://github.com/mateotomas2/numinos/releases/latest) and open it on your phone. Choose a theme, then allow **Show over other apps**.

If Android says this is a restricted setting, open NuminOS in App info, tap the ⋮ menu and choose **Allow restricted settings**.

If the symbol disappears after a while, set the app's battery use to **Unrestricted**. Some phone brands close background apps to save battery.

## Build

You need JDK 17 and the Android SDK.

```sh
./gradlew assembleDebug
```

The APK is written to `app/build/outputs/apk/debug/`.

## Releases

- **Pull request:** publishes a signed pre-release, replaced on every push and removed when the PR closes.
- **Merge to `main`:** publishes the final release. Raise `versionName` in `app/build.gradle.kts` in every PR, or its build fails.
- **Signing:** the pipeline reads the `SIGNING_KEYSTORE_B64` and `SIGNING_PASSWORD` repository secrets. Keep a backup of the key, because Android only installs updates signed with the same key.
